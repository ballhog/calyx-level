# Calyx-Level carrier, rev A: design run-through

(PDF beside it). Board: [`calyx-level-carrier.kicad_pcb`](calyx-level-carrier/calyx-level-carrier.kicad_pcb).
Generated by the scripts in [`tools/`](tools/), which are the calyx-flow carrier tools adapted (same Olimex position,
same Freerouting + DRC flow). Started 2026-10-26.

This is the KiCad version of the bench build in [`docs/schematic.md`](../docs/schematic.md). It keeps the **v1 pin map
exactly**, so the firmware's `pins.h` doesn't change between the proto-shield bench node and this board.

## What it is

One carrier for the **Olimex ESP32-POE-ISO (WROOM)**, plugged onto its EXT1/EXT2 headers, used for every role
(`sensor`, `pump`, `single`); a sensor node just leaves the pump parts off.

- **6 level jacks** (3.5 mm, Apex-style): LVL A, LVL B, RO LOW, LEAK, SPARE 1, SPARE 2.
- **Hardwired high-level cutoff:** the float carries the relay coil current; the relay contact feeds `RAIL`, which is the
  only supply to the pump (+) and the stepstick's VM. No firmware or network in that path.
- **Pump B** (brushed, e.g. the 81 ml/min Kamoer): low-side MOSFET on IO16.
- **Pump A** (stepper, KPHM100/400-ST): TMC2209 stepstick socket, VM from `RAIL`, JST-XH 4 for the Kamoer XHP-4 lead.
- Status LED (IO15), 5 V buzzer (IO2), float-state opto into IO5, LEDs for supply / float OK / pump on.
- Pump supply 12 or 24 V on a barrel jack (Mean Well P1J plug) or a screw terminal. Default build 24 V.

## Design notes

1. **Build options are BOM-only.**
   - **Build B (brushed, default):** R76 fitted, R79 DNP, stepstick socket empty.
   - **Build A (stepper):** R76 DNP (Q1 can be left off), R79 fitted (EN pull-up: driver disabled at boot and when the
     ESP32 is dead), stepstick fitted. The gate pull-down and the EN pull-up can't both sit on IO16, which is why
     one of R76/R79 is always off.
   - **Sensor node:** level parts only; nothing from the pump-supply block onward.
   - **12 V supply:** K1 G5LE-1 DC12, R74 2.2k, R73/R78 4.7k, D2 SMAJ15CA.
2. **High-level cutoff.** Float closed (water below the high mark) → coil powered → contact closes → `RAIL` live.
   High water, a cut float cable or a shorted one (F2 trips) all drop the relay. The relay (mechanical) and Q1 or the
   stepstick (silicon) are in series, so one welded contact or one shorted MOSFET alone can't run the pump. U4 lets
   firmware read the float state; it can't override it.
3. **IO5 reads low while the float is OK**, including at boot. IO5 is a strap that only matters for SDIO timing, so
   that's harmless. IO15 (status LED) low at boot just mutes the ROM boot log.
4. **One ground.** The pump supply ground is the board GND. That's only acceptable because the ISO board isolates the
   Ethernet side, same as the bench notes say. Never build this on the plain ESP32-POE.
5. **Level jacks.** 4-pole jacks so both plug types fit: T = signal, R1 = sensor supply, R2 → GND through a 0R link,
   S = GND. A 3-pole TRS plug's sleeve lands on R2 + S and works as is. If an Apex plug uses R2 for something, remove
   that channel's 0R. **Measure an Apex FMM port before plugging Apex sensors in** (open item in docs/design.md).
   - R1 is fed through 100R so a plug shorting ring to sleeve while it's inserted can't pull the 3V3 rail down
     (50 mA worst case at 5 V). Sensors drawing a few mA lose ~0.5 V across it.
   - Default input: 10k pull-up to 3V3, 1k series, 100 nF (floats, open-collector or 3.3 V outputs).
   - 5 V push-pull sensor: R_PU DNP, R_SER 10k, R_DN 20k → 5 V reads 3.33 V.
6. **Power budget.** The Olimex 5 V comes from its 2 W isolated converter (about 100 mA spare for a carrier). Six
   sensors at a few mA, the status LED and the buzzer (~30 mA while sounding) fit. The relay coil, LEDs on the pump
   side and the pump all run from the pump supply, not from PoE.
7. **Stepper caution.** Don't plug or unplug the motor with `RAIL` live. The float dropping `RAIL` while the motor
   turns is the intended safety action; C3 absorbs the energy, but expect the driver to need a re-enable afterwards.
   TMC2209 VM is 29 V max: a 24 V supply is fine, anything above isn't.
8. **Placement.** 187 × 76 mm, 2 layers, GND pour both sides. Olimex upright on the left as on calyx-flow, RJ45 on the
   bottom edge, antenna overhanging the top edge by about 22 mm. All connectors on the bottom edge: RJ45, six level
   jacks, then the pump side on the right (barrel, supply, float, pump, motor). The 12/24 V parts stay on the right,
   away from the level inputs.
9. **Net classes:** Default 0.25 mm; Power 0.5 mm (GND, +5V, +3V3, SENS_V, float, motor); Pump 0.8 mm, 0.3 mm to
   other nets (VIN, RAIL, PUMP_N). Fine for a ~1.5 A pump on 1 oz copper.

## Status

- **Schematic:** generated; `check_netlist.py` reports 57 intended nets, 0 problems. KiCad 7's CLI has no ERC, so run
  ERC once in KiCad 10.
- **Board:** placed, autorouted (Freerouting, 60 passes: 704 segments, 51 vias), GND pours both sides.
  **DRC: no copper errors and no unconnected items.** Left: 23 silk-to-edge and 3 silk-overlap warnings (cosmetic),
  plus "library not in this configuration" notes from the KiCad 7 build machine.
- **Fab files:** `make_fab.py` writes gerbers, drill, BOM and CPL (45 BOM lines, 39 without an LCSC number yet).
  Renders: [top](calyx-level-carrier/renders/top.png), [bottom](calyx-level-carrier/renders/bottom.png).

### Before ordering

- [ ] **Phoenix MKDS wire-entry side.** The footprints are placed with the +y side on the board edge. Confirm against a
      real MKDS 1,5 (or the 3D model) before ordering.
- [ ] **PJ-320D-4A pinout** against its datasheet (LCSC C95562): which pad is T / R1 / R2 / S, and the nose on the edge.
- [ ] **PC817 SMD lead span** matches `SMDIP-4_W9.53mm` for the part bought.
- [ ] **G5LE-1 pin numbering** (1 COM, 3 NO, 4 NC, 2/5 coil) against the Omron drawing.
- [ ] **Stepstick pinout** of the TMC2209 module bought (EN MS1 MS2 PDN … / VM GND 2B 2A 1A 1B VIO GND).
- [ ] Look over the Freerouting result and tidy the pump-side power tracks by hand.
- [ ] LCSC numbers (most lines have none yet).

## Regenerating

```
python3 hardware/tools/gen_schematic.py              # needs KiCad's symbol library (/usr/share/kicad/symbols)
kicad-cli sch export netlist --format kicadsexpr -o /tmp/cl.net hardware/calyx-level-carrier/calyx-level-carrier.kicad_sch
python3 hardware/tools/check_netlist.py /tmp/cl.net  # 0 problems = every net lands where intended
python3 hardware/tools/gen_pcb.py /tmp/cl.net        # placement, outline, nets (unrouted)
python3 hardware/tools/route_pcb.py                  # Freerouting (under xvfb-run) + GND pours + DRC
python3 hardware/tools/make_fab.py                   # JLC gerbers/drill zip + BOM + CPL
```

KiCad 7 format, so KiCad 10 opens both and upgrades on save. Once either file has been edited by hand in KiCad, stop
regenerating it: the KiCad file is then the source.
